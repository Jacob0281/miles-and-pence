Miles & Pence price collector (Windows)
=======================================

The government Fuel Finder API only answers connections from the UK, so this small
add-on collects prices from your computer and sends them to the Miles & Pence site.

Setup: double-click Setup.cmd and paste your Fuel Finder Client ID, Client Secret
and GitHub token when asked. It runs a first fetch, then every 30 minutes while
this computer is on.

Log:       %LOCALAPPDATA%\MilesAndPence\collector.log
Remove:    double-click Uninstall.cmd

Fuel price data: UK Government Fuel Finder scheme, Open Government Licence v3.0.
